# MARS Tablet required information

- Surface PNT (Positioning, Navigation, Timing). Will need some maths equations to calculate exact locations, as cannot completely rely on satellite and compass.
- Date Time. Show for both Earth and Mars (Sol Number, Local Mean Solar Time).
- Geo Tagging. Putting coordinates at the captured photo and equipment readings.
- Mineral Signatures
- Route Planning, Satellite orbit path, previous missions route.
- Key exploration Land Marks.
- Teammate location (Buddy tracking, rule of 2)
- Reverse Breadcrumb tracking (Backtracking)


- Orbital Relay Window. Countdown time to the next overhead satellite pass.
- Estimated time till receiving message from ground base. (distinguish between Local Surface Base and Earth Mission Control)
- Life Support Details:
	- Primary & Reserve O2: Tank Pressure, mass flow rate, remaining endurance time
	- CO2 Srubber Capacity: Saturation percentage, Partial pressure of CO2
	- Suit Battery & Thermal Control Water: Remaining kWh, remaining L
	- Metabolic Rate: Heart rate, respiratory rate, calories burn
- Point of No Return. Spatial boundary calculation
-  Micro-Topology & Slope Heatmap
- Solar Azimuth Compass. Derived from local time, latitude and sun sensors
- RF Line-of-Sight & Terrain Masking
- Regolith Trafficability & Slip Index. Granular soil risk layers derived from THEMIS
- Rock Abundance & Obstacle Density. Boulder where size exceed suit step over clearance
- Drop-Off Alerts. Alarms when approaching cliff edge, crater rim or unstable land.
- Atmospheric & Space Weather:
	- Surface Pressure
	- Wind Vectors (Directions)
	- Dust Optical Depth
	- Solar Flux
	- Ionizing Radiation Rate
